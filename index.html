about

whoami

I started out running networks and IT operations — configuring MikroTik and Cisco gear, managing Windows AD and Microsoft 365, and keeping infrastructure across offices online. That work took me from a cybersecurity analyst internship through IT officer and IT specialist roles in Dhaka, Bangladesh, before I moved to Turkey on a Türkiye Bursları scholarship to pursue a master's in Computer Engineering.

Alongside operations work, I've published research on low-cost robotics — including the PAINTIQUE spray-painting robot — and I'm now applying the same systems thinking to deep learning, including a U-NET based brain tumor detection project. I like problems that sit at the boundary of infrastructure and intelligence: the network that has to stay up, and the model that has to be trained on it.
